⌨️ Transfer command

Fast command

Format: @player1 10000 to @player2
Names are matched without case sensitivity. A transfer subtracts from the sender and adds the same amount to the receiver. Spaces in names are supported.

Example: @Asha 10000 to @Ravi transfers ₹10,000 from Asha to Ravi. Negative account balances are allowed only after you confirm the warning.

🏦 Loans

5% per round
Game rule: each round, 5% of the loan's original amount is deducted from the borrower's account and reduces the remaining loan balance. Example: a ₹10,000 loan pays ₹500 per round; after 2 rounds, ₹9,000 remains. If the account lacks cash, it can go negative so the round still advances.
